Aurora Obsidian
The Liquid Obsidian structure — its glass recipe, specular rim lighting, premium restraint, and pill buttons — now wearing the Aurora Pulse colour palette. Void black with a teal undertone. Cyan cuts. Violet earns.
Colour palette
What changed from Liquid Obsidian
Void colour — pure magenta-black (#04020A) → teal-black (#030A0C). The background now carries a barely-perceptible cyan undertone, so the ambient aurora glows feel native to the space rather than imposed on it.

Primary accent — magenta (#E879F9) → electric cyan (#00BCD4 / #4DD0E1). Cyan is the dominant data colour. More focused, more "flow state."

Secondary accent — violet (#A78BFA) → aurora violet-magenta (#CE93D8 / #9C88B8). Reserved strictly for the weekly ring and elevation — the "earned" moments.
Glass recipe (unchanged from Liquid Obsidian)
3-layer shell — backdrop-blur(32px) saturate(190%) base · specular gradient sweep (bright top-left → transparent) · inset rim: 1.5px white top edge, 1px dark bottom edge.

Border gradient ring — 1px gradient from rgba(0,188,212,0.32) top-left to rgba(206,147,216,0.14) bottom-right. Never a flat line.

Buttons — same 3-layer pill. Stronger specular (0.58α top highlight). Heartbeat glow animation in cyan every 4s. Press: scale(0.97) + shadow collapses inward.
Animation
Aurora drift — two radial orbs (cyan + violet-magenta) on independent 11s/8s sine paths. Imperceptible in motion, felt as warmth.

Specular shimmer — the highlight gradient on each card sweeps very slowly left-to-right (9s loop). Looks like light crossing glass.

PR shockwave — personal record triggers a single cyan ring expanding from the metric card. Once. Fades in 0.9s. Earned, not constant.

Micro-refraction — on touch, specular shifts position slightly. Physical glass under light.
